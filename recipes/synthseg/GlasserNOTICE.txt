Original cortical parcellation:
Glasser MF et al. A multi-modal parcellation of human cerebral cortex.
Nature 536, 171-178 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1038/nature18933

This container changes the shared hemisphere IDs of the distributed volume.
Original IDs 1..180 are retained at physical MNI RAS x<=0. At x>0, 180 is
added, giving right IDs 181..360. Labelled x=0 voxels belong to the left.
Right parcel names are derived by replacing L_ with R_ in the published
left parcel table. The resulting table is /opt/glasser/labels.txt.

The optional native-volume output is an approximate spatial registration,
clipped to SynthSeg cortical grey matter. It is not an individual-subject
HCP multimodal surface parcellation.
